### **The context**

Finance, money, payments, transactions — these are all evolving as fintech develops. Movement of money is evolving, becoming more and more continuous rather than discrete. What are the incentives behind this change?

**Merchants want to reduce friction for customers**

Merchants want it to be easy to purchase things. This means merchants will pay the premium for credit cards, BNPL, POS systems, etc. to minimize friction from intent to payment. And consumers will gladly adopt these!

This occurs the most for merchants that sell non-essential goods or face competition. Their demand rises if they’re able to offer more value to consumers with easy payment methods. If you’re a monopoly selling water, you can feel free to require cash all you want.

Regardless, all merchants are able to increase consumer purchasing power by offering financing. More customers will buy your fridge if they’re able to purchase it on the spot instead of waiting until their next few paychecks.

**Employers and governments want to offer faster cashflow**

1. Workers want to be paid faster, and **employers** want to attract and retain workers. HR teams will adopt earned wage systems as an employee perk (much better for workers than payday loans!)
2. Businesses want to quicken their cashflow, and **governments** want to help these businesses and grow country GDP. This is why the U.S. government is adopting FedNow and pushing for other regulation to expedite ACH transfers and wires.

These changes generally occur at the limits of the socioeconomic spectrum. If workers and businesses were all wealthy, they wouldn’t need cashflow efficiencies. However, there exists scarcity in this world, so there are plenty of stakeholders that care about receiving an invoice or paycheck days, or hours, earlier if possible.

### What the future might look like

Given these forces increasing transaction velocity, here’s some (optimistic) ideas on what the future will look like:

**Instant, global payment rails x perfect salary streaming**

People across the world will transact on blockchain rails, with a global ledger managing the ownership of liquid assets. Income will be near-perfectly efficient.

- Hire someone abroad for $10 to ghostwrite a media post for you, no marketplace or intermediary needed.
- A migrant worker can cover his mother’s every meal with every hour he works.

**Portable identity layer x agentic AI**

With higher stakes in authorized money movement (instant irreversible payments), usernames and passwords won’t be secure enough. The most secure and smoothest authorization method is through our personal device footprint or biometric data.

- Open your phone with FaceID, then open up any app or website with no login needed. No more forgetting your password.
- Your phone knows you — it knows your transactions, product activity, etc. across all your accounts and can fully personalize your AI chat.
- Tell Siri “book me a flight home” or “order me lunch”, and it can execute.

**Real-time card controls x AI budgeting**

Transactions will all be programmatic — conditional on if they aren’t fraudulent, and also on any smart rails you’d like to set up.

- Stop worrying about whether you’ve spent too much, and just swipe your card on a purchase to check. Your banking app will check with you first, if it detects you’re not on track to cover your upcoming payments.
- At the start of the month, call with your budgeting assistant about what you want to spend money on this month. It’ll automatically set up your budget and stop you from spending money on the wrong things.

**Embedded lending x perfect credit risk models**

Lending will be fully coupled with POS and loans will be instantly approved, resulting in large purchases being instantly financed and executed. (corollary: loan negotiation will become defunct with AI negotiators, so no back-and-forth needed.)

- Scroll on Zillow, click on a home, tour the home with VR, think a bit, then click purchase. Your mortgage terms are perfectly fit for you, with no down payment required.
- Crippling student debt will become a thing of the past. Aspiring college students looking to pursue certain careers will be nudged into the right direction (different college or different career) instead of being approved for a loan they won’t be able to pay back.

**Embedded financing x browsy shopping**

AI will purchase everything it knows you’d usually buy, and if you have something specific to purchase, you’ll let your AI know. The only human shopping remaining will be the fun type. Similar to what happened to in-person bookstores, stores will become browsy — only for looking around, with no agenda. And financing will be available at checkout whenever needed.

- Redo your entire wardrobe during a winter weekend with nothing else to do. BNPL with a timeline of 1 year, 2 years, or however long you need.
- Scroll through tiktok and long-click on any item in each video to auto-purchase.

**Embedded finance x social bots and AI pricing**

Bots (good bots!) will be part of our content streams and group chats, providing useful contributions personalized to the audience. Payments will be embedded into any and all platforms, enabling paywalls or money movement wherever. Pricing will be fully dynamic, like Uber’s but better, and AI generated.

- Twitter post or medium article, with AI pricing based on how much you’ll like it. Tap to pay and view.
- Your friend groupchat bot suggests that you all take a weekend trip to Miami. React to the message; once three friends react, everything is paid and booked.
- Concert-bot posts on your feed about an upcoming concert for an artist you like. Once you and a couple friends like the same post, it’ll book tickets for all three of you and put you in a group chat.

### Final words

With finance-enabled AGI comes a shift in the way our money is spent. Ideally, our spending approaches what is best for our long-term wealth and personal fulfillment, with some room for joyful purchases as well.
